About Us

Made in India,
built for the trade.

GA Demands is a Made in India diamond trading platform built to help manufacturers, traders, brokers, and jewellers buy and sell diamonds faster and more efficiently.

GA Demands was co-founded by Prithvi Manilal, Akash Manilal, and Nicole Manilal, the fourth generation of a diamantaire family.

Their grandfather, Prasanbhai Manilal Jhaveri, was one of the early diamantaires who helped bring international trade into the Indian diamond industry and played a key role in establishing India's relationship with Argyle. He was also a De Beers Sight Holder, Argyle Sightholder, and ALROSA Alliance Holder.

Drawing upon generations of industry knowledge and relationships, the team behind GA Demands is committed to helping the diamond trade embrace technology while preserving the trust and connections that have always been at the heart of the industry.

Our mission is simple: help the diamond industry buy and sell diamonds instantly.
