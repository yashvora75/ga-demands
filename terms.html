3. GA DEMANDS APPLICATION AND SERVICES

GA Demands Application is a downloadable application through the app store / google play store, which is an integration application which provides an interface to the interested manufacturers, traders, retailers, buyers, sellers and brokers of Diamonds to connect online for trading in Diamonds offline and also provides features like calculator for computing the Diamond cost, publish newsletters and articles in respect to gem and jewellery industry and host knowledge sharing sessions.

Services offered to the Users of GA Demands Application shall, inter alia, be in the nature of the following services including any new features and services that may be added from time to time (collectively, the “Services”):

Post Now icon: The Users have the opportunity to post their demand by putting in all the details of the demand for Diamonds, including with respect to whether the demand for the Diamonds is for a parcel (P), single certified (SC) or single non-certified (S) diamond (“Demand”). Once the User clicks on any of these aforesaid options, they are directed to the page where they can put in all specific details of their Demand (which are predefined values based on the diamond industry, as well as terms and remarks box with free text), the GA Demands Application also matches the Demand to inventory details.

View / Filter / Star / Save icon: The Users have the opportunity to do the following action –

For viewing: Users need to click on a Demand tab to view the details of the Diamond requirement.

For Filter: Users need to click on sell button and select from which Demands they want to filter (parcel (P), single certified (SC) and single non-certified (S)) and then select the Diamond details that relate to them.

For Star/Save: Users can click on star button to save Demands for future reference and for tracking them if they would like to in future.

Call / Message / Share Feature: Users have the facility to message, call or share the Demand. The message and share button are visible in the Demand page. Users can share text messages, images, videos, voice notes and documents in the message section. When the Users click on the profile page of the buyer, they can view other active Demands the buyer has posted, the details of the buyer (first name, last name, company name, email, bio, company address, member since) as well as the call button to call the buyer.

Report icon: In the event any Demand is wrongly listed or there is any other issue / query related to other User, the User can report a particular Demand by clicking on the Report icon set out on the Demand page or on user profile page. Also, in the event any Inventory is wrongly listed or there is any other issue / query user can report a particular Inventory by clicking on the Report icon set out on the Inventory Page.

Calculate Now icon: The Users have the opportunity to use the calculator feature on the GA Demands Application, where they can easily calculate values of the Diamond deals they are doing with fields like, including with respect to carat(s), price/carat (USD/INR), terms, due, conversation rate (rate of USD to INR), brokerage. The Users shall get the output of total amount (either in USD or INR based on the specific User’s choice) as well as the brokerage amount and net amount of the business value. It is expressly provided that the calculations are for information purpose and the Company does not warrant the veracity and authenticity of the same as the same are mere estimates based on independent opinion and computation methodology of the Company.

Learn More icon: The Users will have the opportunity to access the latest News, Webinars and Articles in the knowledge section. This section consists of 3 (three) parts and the Users may make unlimited use of the same:

News: GA Demands Application also aggregates real time news from various industry sources present it to Users. When the Users click on an article title, it redirects the User to the website/news article of that publication.

Blogs: GA Demands Application has a blog section wherein the blogs by GemAtlas are published periodically.

Webinars: GA Demands Application past webinars (and showcase them through youtube).

FAQs: Frequently asked questions (“FAQs”) are most commonly asked questions that the Company on best effort basis tries to solve for the Users. The FAQs shall solve some of the most common issues that the User might face in understanding about the GA Demands Application and the features provided by GA Demands Application as available under https://gademands.com/.

Inventory: The Users have the opportunity to upload their inventory by putting in all the details of the inventory for Diamonds, including with respect to whether the inventory for the Diamonds is for a parcel (P), single certified (SC) or single non-certified (S) diamond (“Inventory”). Once the User clicks on any of these aforesaid options, they are directed to the page where they can put in all specific details of their Inventory (which are predefined values based on the diamond industry, as well as terms and remarks box with free text), the GA Demands Application also matches the Inventory to Demands details.

Suggestions: The Users on providing consent to the Company on the GA Demand Application to have access to the contacts on their mobile number grants the Company the right to provide suggestions to the Users on certain mutual connects who may also be using the GA Demands Application.
